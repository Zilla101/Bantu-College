Administrative Deliverables & Client Requirements

Checklist of assets, credentials, and institutional policies needed from Bantu College

To transition the deployed platform from local institutional testing to full live cloud synchronization, automated email delivery, and active payment gateway settlements, Wafers Engineering requires the following deliverables from Bantu College leadership:

Required Item Description & Purpose
1. Official School Email & SMTP Designated institutional admissions email address (e.g. admissions@bantucollege.edu) and SMTP/app credentials so automated examination dockets and admission letters land directly in applicants' email inboxes.
2. Official School Bank Account Details Corporate bank account name, bank name, and account number to embed on official fee schedules, provisional admission offer letters, and bank payment invoices.
3. Payment Gateway API Key (Optional) Registered Paystack or Flutterwave merchant public key (pk_live_...) if the school wishes to accept direct online tuition payments via debit card, USSD, and bank transfer.
4. Principal's Signature & Official Stamp High-resolution transparent PNG scan of Principal Mr. Kwaghtongo D. A.'s signature and the school's circular rubber stamp to replace placeholders on official A4 Report Cards, Admission Letters, and Bursary Receipts.
5. Confirmed Fee & Sundry Schedule Final breakdown of tuition per term/session across Junior (Basic 7-9) and Senior (SS1-3) cohorts, including sundry charges: acceptance fee, uniform kit, textbook levies, development fund, and PTA dues.
6. Academic Calendar & Entrance Dates Confirmed dates, time, and venue for the 2026/2027 Entrance Screening Assessment, screening subject papers, and official resumption dates.
7. Domain Name & Production Hosting Registered institutional domain name (e.g. bantucollege.edu.ng) and access credentials to the school's cPanel or cloud hosting server for live deployment.

Terms & Handover Conditions

  1. The delivered architecture encompasses all nine functional modules outlined in the Scope of Work table.
  2. Integration of client-provided assets (email credentials, bank details, signatures, and domain) will be executed immediately upon receipt at no additional engineering charge.
  3. Two rounds of administrative adjustments and workflow fine-tuning are included in this milestone.
  4. Upon final settlement and project sign-off, full source code, database architecture, and design assets belong exclusively to Bantu College.
  5. Wafers Engineering provides 30 calendar days of post-handover technical support, maintenance, and bug rectification at no additional cost.
