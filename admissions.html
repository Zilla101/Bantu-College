Admissions

Parents and guardians who wish to enroll their children into Bantu College should review our entry requirements and complete the registration form below.

Classes and Entry Qualifications

Basic 7 (JSS 1)
Basic 8 (JSS 2)

Candidate Requirements

A prospective student must be at least 11 years of age and must have completed primary education up to Basic 6 (Primary 6) before admission can be confirmed.

Admissions Steps

01.

Submit Application

Complete the online form below or pick up physical application documents at the school administrative desk.

02.

Entrance Examination

Applicants sit for written papers in Mathematics, English Studies, and General Aptitude appropriate for their chosen class.

03.

Interview and Verification

Shortlisted candidates and their parents attend a brief in person interview with Mr. Kwaghtongo D. A. and senior staff members.

Fees and Common Questions

What is the school tuition fee? +
Tuition covers regular academic instruction, science lab use, and computer room access. For the 2026/2027 academic session, tuition is ₦450,000 per session. Boarding, uniforms, and textbooks are billed separately.
What is the age requirement for admission? +
Students must have reached at least 11 years of age by their admission date and must present proof of completing Primary 6.
When do applications close? +
Admission forms are processed as they are received. We advise early registration because spaces in Basic 7 and Basic 8 are strictly limited to keep class sizes manageable.
