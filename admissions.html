Mandatory Entry Qualification

Prospective students must be at least 11 years of age and must have successfully completed their middle basic education (Basic 6 / Primary 6) with demonstrable academic readiness.

The Admissions Process

01.

Application Submission

Submit your formal online application alongside academic records and Basic 6 completion credentials.

02.

Entrance Examination

Candidates complete rigorous entrance examinations in Mathematics, English Studies, and General Aptitude tailored for Basic 7 and Basic 8 intake.

03.

Panel Interview

A detailed panel interaction with the prospective scholar and guardian to align on core character values, discipline, and educational objectives.

Tuition & FAQ

What is the annual tuition fee? +
Tuition covers core academic instruction, digital learning resources, and use of campus facilities across our 10-acre grounds. For the 2026/2027 academic session, core tuition is ₦450,000 per session. Laboratory, extracurricular, and uniform fees apply separately.
What ages are accepted for admission? +
Applicants must be at least 11 years of age upon admission and have completed Basic 6 (Primary 6). We currently enroll students exclusively into Basic 7 (JSS 1) and Basic 8 (JSS 2).
When is the application deadline? +
Applications for the upcoming session are reviewed on a rolling basis. Early submission is highly encouraged due to limited cohort capacity in Basic 7 & 8.
