Academic Curriculum

At Bantu College, our academic curriculum combines rigorous scholarship with cultural depth and technological forward-thinking. We prepare every scholar to lead with intellectual clarity and integrity.

Classes Offered

We are currently enrolling driven and inquisitive students for our foundational junior secondary levels:

● Basic 7 (JSS 1)
● Basic 8 (JSS 2)

Entry Qualification

Applicants must be at least 11 years of age and must have successfully completed middle basic education (Basic 6).

Curriculum Pillars

Our holistic educational framework is structured around three key pillars that balance academic mastery, creativity, and global communication:

Core Learning

  • Mathematics: Foundational logic, quantitative reasoning, problem solving, and analytical mastery.
  • English: Comprehensive literacy and communication, deeply integrated with Nigerian and African Studies.
  • Integrated Science: Hands-on inquiry, scientific methodology, physics, chemistry, and biology foundations.
  • Integrated Humanities: Rigorous examination of Geography and Nigerian/World History.

Creative & Active Learning

  • Fine Art and Design: Visual expression, design thinking, aesthetic appreciation, and craftsmanship.
  • Physical Education (PE): Athletics, fitness, teamwork, discipline, and personal health.
  • Music: Theory, instrumental performance, vocal discipline, and cultural musical heritage.
  • Coding: Computational logic, introductory programming, algorithms, and digital literacy.

Languages & Culture

  • PSHE: Personal, Social, and Health Education for moral character and leadership.
  • French: Francophone linguistic competence and international communication.
  • Chinese: Mandarin language acquisition opening doors to global trade and diplomacy.
  • Tiv Language: Cultural preservation, indigenous linguistics, and ancestral pride.

World-Class Teachers: Evidence-Based Learning

At Bantu College, our educators don't rely on rote memorization. Under the pedagogical leadership of Mr. Terna Iwar, our faculty employs proven, evidence-based instructional methodologies that monitor individual student progression, encourage active critical inquiry, and cultivate independent mastery.

Structured Inquiry

Students are taught how to question evidence, formulate hypotheses, and present rigorous arguments.

Data-Informed Progress

Continuous diagnostic assessments pinpoint learning gaps early, tailoring personal support for every scholar.

Global Perspective

Rooted in Nigerian values while incorporating international languages and modern coding technologies.
