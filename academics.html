Academic Curriculum

Our academic work is planned to give junior secondary students a solid grounding in reading, writing, mathematical computation, scientific experiments, and moral responsibility.

Classes Currently Offered

We are currently enrolling students into our junior secondary classes:

Basic 7 (JSS 1)
Basic 8 (JSS 2)

Entry Requirements

Applicants must be at least 11 years of age and must have completed middle basic education (Basic 6 or Primary 6) from a recognized primary school.

Curriculum Areas

Our curriculum is divided into three key learning areas:

Core Learning

  • Mathematics: Arithmetic, algebra, geometry, word problems, and logical calculation.
  • English: Grammar, comprehension, essay writing, and Nigerian and African literature.
  • Integrated Science: Practical introduction to biological, physical, and chemical concepts.
  • Integrated Humanities: Nigerian history, African geography, and citizenship education.

Creative and Active Learning

  • Fine Art and Design: Drawing, painting, local crafts, and design fundamentals.
  • Physical Education: Athletics, ball games, physical fitness, and health education.
  • Music: Basic music theory, voice training, and Nigerian musical traditions.
  • Coding: Computer operations, typing, and introductory programming logic.

Languages and Culture

  • PSHE: Personal, social, and health education covering moral conduct and hygiene.
  • French: Basic speaking, listening, and reading in French.
  • Chinese: Introduction to Mandarin vocabulary, pronunciation, and writing.
  • Tiv Language: Reading, writing, and speaking in Tiv language and cultural appreciation.

Teaching Methods and Continuous Assessment

Under the educational leadership of Mr. Kwaghtongo D. A., our teachers rely on proven classroom teaching methods. Lessons are broken into clear steps, followed by practical classroom exercises, homework checks, and weekly quizzes to make sure students actually understand what is being taught.

Careful Instruction

Teachers explain topics thoroughly and guide students through sample questions before setting independent tasks.

Regular Progress Checks

Continuous assessment scores and termly reports keep parents fully informed about their child's academic standing.

Practical Application

Classroom theory is balanced with real laboratory work, computer usage, and physical sports.
